Follow the instructions one step at a time.

Step 1

Open the ECAE Website or Portal

  1. Open the Emirates College for Advanced Education (ECAE) website.
  2. Click Login in the top-right corner.
  3. Select ECAE Portal.
  4. You can also open the portal directly using the links below.
Open ECAE Website Open ECAE Portal
Step 2

Sign In and Select Students

  1. Sign in using your ECAE username and password.
  2. After signing in, select the Students tab.
If you cannot access the portal, reset your password through Office 365 and try again.

Important:
Students using other corporate devices may need to sign in using the following username format:

ECAE\StudentID
Example: ECAE\001164
Open Office 365
Step 3

Open Kottab Learning Management System

  1. After selecting the Students tab, look at the right-side panel.
  2. Find the Important Links (Internal) section.
  3. Select Kottab Learning Management System .
Step 4

Access Kottab LMS

  1. Open the Kottab Learning Management System.
  2. Sign in using your ECAE username and password.
  3. Access course materials, assignments, quizzes, discussions, and announcements.
  4. Review the information provided by your instructors.
The Kottab Learning Management System (LMS) is the primary online learning platform used by ECAE.
If you are unable to access Kottab, your password may have expired. Reset your password through Office 365 and try again.
